Exam Instructions

  1. Read each question carefully before selecting your answer.
  2. The remaining exam time is displayed on the screen. Keep an eye on it.
  3. Question status is indicated by the color of the corresponding box:
    • Green: Answered and saved.
    • Red: Not answered.
    • White: Not visited yet.
  4. Before submitting the exam, return to all questions marked for review, verify your answers, remove the "Mark for Review" status, and ensure that the answers are saved.
  5. You may change your answer at any time before ending the exam. If you modify an answer, you must click "Save" again.
  6. The "End Test" button will appear after half of the exam duration has elapsed.
  7. Before ending the test, make sure that all questions have been answered and saved successfully.
  8. Once the exam has been ended, no further changes to your answers will be allowed.
